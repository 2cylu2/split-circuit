# Split Circuit

Split Circuit is a platforming game developed in Unreal Engine
for a video game development class.

## Gameplay

Navigate across platforms designed like disconnected circuits while
avoiding red wires. Falling or touching a red wire sends the player
back to the start.

Collect yellow energy balls to progress through increasingly
challenging levels. In the final level, collect enough energy to
activate the escape button and complete the game.

## Features

- Main menu and pause menu
- Resume, restart, and quit functionality
- Circuit-themed platforming
- Red wire hazards that reset the player
- Collectible energy
- Three progressively challenging levels
- Final escape objective
